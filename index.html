Good to know

Quick answers

Where is The O Spot?

The O Spot by Chef Jun is at W Plaza Commercial. Use the Google Maps link for directions.

What time is The O Spot open?

The O Spot is open from 12 noon to 12 midnight.

How can I contact The O Spot?

Call or text 0995 937 1753, or message the Facebook page.

How much is a bucket of beer at The O Spot?

The Mix & Match Beer Bucket of 5 is ₱390. That is ₱35 less than buying 5 bottles at ₱85 each.

What beers can I choose for the beer bucket?

You can mix any 5 bottles of San Miguel Light, San Miguel Flavored Beer (Apple), San Miguel Pale Pilsen, San Mig Super Dry and Red Horse.

How much is one bottle of beer?

A single bottle of beer is ₱85.

What is the cheapest food on the menu?

Chicken Poppers, French Fries and Cheese Stick are ₱99 each.

Do you serve sisig and lechon kawali?

Yes. Sizzling Pork Sisig and Lechon Kawali are ₱149 each.

Can I add rice to my order?

Yes. Rice is available as an add-on for ₱20.

Do you have non-alcoholic drinks?

Yes. Softdrinks (Coke, Sprite or Royal) are ₱25 and mineral water is ₱20.
